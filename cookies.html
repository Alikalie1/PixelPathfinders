Cookie Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

This policy explains the cookies and browser storage used by the Pixel Pathfinders website. You can change optional storage at any time with the Cookie settings button.

Essential storage

Supabase authentication uses browser storage to keep you signed in to your member or administrator account. We also store your cookie preference so the site can remember your choice. These functions are required for the account and preference features you request.

Optional functional preferences

If you allow functional preferences, the site remembers your accessibility display settings and whether you completed a member or administrator dashboard tour. If you choose essential storage only, these preferences apply for the current visit and are not saved for later visits.

Analytics and advertising

Pixel Pathfinders does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies, and does not use browser storage for cross-site advertising.

Third-party resources

Pages may request fonts, icons, and software libraries from providers such as Google Fonts, Cloudflare, and jsDelivr. Supabase provides authentication and database services. Those providers receive connection information when your browser requests their services; their own privacy policies describe their handling of that information. We do not use those requests for advertising analytics.

Managing your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the site to allow or turn off optional functional preferences. Essential account storage remains active. You can also clear browser storage in your browser settings; doing so may sign you out.

Questions

For questions about this policy, contact us through the Contact page. See our Privacy Policy for information about personal data.
